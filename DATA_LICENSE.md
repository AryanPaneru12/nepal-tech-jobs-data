# Data license

Original database structure and facts curated by this project are made available under the
[Open Data Commons Attribution License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/).

This license does not override rights or restrictions attached to upstream sources. Every export
includes `source_id`, `source_url`, and attribution fields. OpenStreetMap-derived records, if added,
must be exported separately under ODbL. Raw pages, restricted descriptions, personal recruiter
details, and Google Places content other than durable place IDs are not public dataset content.
