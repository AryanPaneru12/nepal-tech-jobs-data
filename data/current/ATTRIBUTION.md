# Source attribution

- **Ashby public Job Postings API:** First-party employer posting served by Ashby. (https://developers.ashbyhq.com/docs/public-job-posting-api)
- **Brave Web Search discovery:** Brave Search is used for discovery; every company requires independent verification. (https://api-dashboard.search.brave.com/terms-of-service)
- **Google Places ID-only discovery:** Google Maps; only durable Place IDs are retained. (https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/places/web-service/policies)
- **Greenhouse public Job Board API:** First-party employer posting served by Greenhouse. (https://docs.greenhouse.io/job-board.html)
- **Himalayas remote jobs API:** Himalayas — mention Himalayas and link to the source listing. (https://himalayas.app/api)
- **Lever public Postings API:** First-party employer posting served by Lever. (https://github.com/lever/postings-api)
- **NAS-IT member directory:** Nepal Association for Software and IT Services Companies (NAS-IT). (https://nasit.org.np/)
- **Nepal directories and job boards pending source-specific review:** Source-specific attribution is required before enabling any adapter. (manual source)
- **Nepal Office of Company Registrar manual export:** Office of Company Registrar, Government of Nepal. (https://ocr.gov.np/)
- **Remote OK public jobs feed:** Remote OK — link to the original Remote OK job page. (https://remoteok.com/legal)
- **Remotive public jobs API:** Remotive — link back to the Remotive listing; feed is delayed by 24 hours. (https://github.com/remotive-io/remote-jobs-api)
